# NNNN. Title in the imperative, stating the decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD (the date the decision was made, from the build brief or the git log)
- **Deciders:** blairforce1; implemented by Claude Opus 5 in Claude Code
- **Supersedes:** none | NNNN

## Context

Why the decision was needed: the forces, the constraints, what breaks without it. Facts, not history. At most ten lines.

## Decision

Active voice, present tense: "kustofmt does X." The decision itself, with the one reason that carried it. At most fifteen lines.

## Considered options

- **Option name.** One line: what it was and why it lost.

## Consequences

- Easier: ...
- Harder: ...
- Follow-up: ...

Honest negatives are required.

## Where it is enforced

The file, test, target or workflow that makes the decision true. Cross-references to other records as `decisions/NNNN`.
